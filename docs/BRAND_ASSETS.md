The cover and social card reuse the repository’s original `public/launch-crew.svg` rocket, comic-space palette and star mark. SVG compositions are the editable sources; PNG exports were rendered with resvg-js using locally installed Impact and Arial fonts. No font files are redistributed. The favicon extends the existing lime star mark. These are code-native vector assets; no image-generation model was used.

Art direction: dark starfield and violet/blue nebulae, cream condensed title, pink accents, lime star and jelly pilot, outlined planets, and the copy “Explore alien worlds. Sign the big decisions.” Keep the title, mascot and device attribution inside the social-card crop.

The website declares the absolute Open Graph image URL and favicon links in `public/index.html`. Submission copy is in [HACKATHON_SUBMISSION.md](HACKATHON_SUBMISSION.md).
